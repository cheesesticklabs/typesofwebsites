## Slide 2 — Part 1: Explain the design

**What is this website’s main purpose?**

Information, entertainment, or sales?

Find one piece of evidence from **each** part of CSSA:

- **Content:** What the site includes.
- **Structure:** How it is organized.
- **Style:** How it looks.
- **Accessibility:** How people can access and use it.

**Everyone makes four annotations.**

In each comment, identify the feature, select its CSSA category, and explain how it supports the site’s purpose.

Teacher note: Allow 3 minutes to explore and agree on purpose, then 9 minutes for evidence and explanations. Students can agree on the same features but each needs their own annotated record.

## Slide 3 — Part 2: Improve the website

**Your group has been hired to improve this site.**

Choose one specific change that would help it accomplish its purpose.

Everyone adds a fifth annotation: **“Our improvement.”**

- What would you change?
- Which CSSA category does it affect?
- What would it help visitors do?
- Why would that better support the purpose?

Teacher note: This is a written proposal attached to the relevant part of the site. Students do not edit the website. If a group is vague, ask: “Exactly what would look, work, or appear differently?” A possible spoken example: “Add a difficulty label beside each puzzle so visitors can choose a game they’ll enjoy.”

## Slide 4 — Your 20-minute work plan

| Time | Task |
|---|---|
| 3 minutes | Explore. Agree on the purpose. |
| 9 minutes | Make four explained CSSA annotations. |
| 5 minutes | Propose and annotate one improvement. |
| 3 minutes | Choose your strongest evidence. Prepare to share. |

**Before you finish:**

Four evidence annotations + one improvement annotation + three written answers.

**Your group will share one strongest piece of existing evidence and one proposed improvement.**

Teacher note: Keep this slide visible during work. Check progress around minutes 3, 12, and 17. The class share-out begins after the 20-minute work period.

## Slide 5 — Show us your strongest evidence

**Presenting group:**

1. Name your website and its purpose.
2. Show your strongest feature. Identify its CSSA category and explain your reasoning.
3. Describe your proposed improvement and how it would help visitors.

**Everyone else:**

Open the presenting group’s site on **Discuss**. Follow their evidence and listen for the connection between design and purpose.

Teacher note: Give the Discuss password, 300. Allow about one minute per group for eight groups. Students show the feature in the clean gallery; their individual pins stay on their own investigation pages.
